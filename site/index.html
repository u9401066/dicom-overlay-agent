01

One image, several accountable passes

The product loop is more than one model call against one screenshot.

  1. Capture ROI

    The configured screen region is captured in physical pixels after PHI margins are applied.

  2. Multi-pass inspect

    Coarse findings nominate bounded crop/refine targets, then a final pass reconciles the evidence.

  3. Human review

    The reviewer can inspect or draw a region, ask crop-scoped QA, and explicitly apply or dismiss a structured report update.

04

Safety and scope

Useful assistance depends on visible limits, not hidden certainty.

A

Physician final call

The agent supports review. It does not diagnose autonomously or replace clinical judgment.

B

Screenshot incompleteness

Lead inventory and image quality gates limit claims when the captured view lacks context.

C

External waveform evidence

ECGFounder scores are provenance-bound and remain supporting evidence until calibrated.

D

Configured ROI only

The capture is constrained to user-defined physical screen margins before remote analysis.

05

Build and inspect the MVP

The portable Windows bundle includes the Python/Qt app, pinned OpenClaw runtime, plugin, and modality skills. Torch, checkpoints, MEETI data, secrets, and ECGFounder sidecar assets stay outside the bundle.
